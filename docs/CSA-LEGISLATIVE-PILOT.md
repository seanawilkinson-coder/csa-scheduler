# CSA Legislative Cycle Pilot

Status: executable pilot for the 2026–27 Annual Legislative Cycle.

This pilot exercises the portal with representative records before CSA governance rules are frozen. Dates are configurable cycle data; they are not a substitute for the Commissioner's approved notice.

## Configured policy

- Policy version: v4
- Voting units: Columbia, Princeton, Penn, Trinity, Yale
- Quorum: 3 represented voting units
- Approval threshold: 50% of decided ballots
- Abstentions: recorded separately; excluded from the yes/no denominator
- Proxy registration window: 72 hours before the relevant vote or meeting
- Review and access changes: Commissioner or explicitly assigned legislative administrator

## Pilot records

| Record | Route | Pilot question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| p1 | Bucket 3 · comment window | Can participants discuss a distributed record, create a revision, and preserve the earlier version? |
| p2 | Bucket 2 · staff review | Can undistributed material remain private to the proposer and governance staff? |
| p3 | Bucket 3 · coach vote | Are ballots scoped to program voting units, confidential before close, and routed to the Board after a passing result? |
| p4 | Bucket 1 · adopted | Does an administrative decision retain its reason and effective date? |
| p5 | Bucket 2 · comment window | Can a conference observer comment without gaining edit, access, or voting authority? |

## Run of show

1. Commissioner confirms the cycle dates and active policy version.
2. One coach signs in and verifies that only the authorized program account is available.
3. Coaches comment on p1; the proposer creates a revision; staff reassigns and redistributes it.
4. Staff opens p3 for voting; three program units submit ballots; staff finalizes; a Board member records the outcome.
5. A coach, Board member, staff member, and observer each open the same distributed record and verify their action boundary.
6. Staff downloads the p1 and p3 record packets and checks the version history, discussion, decision, and audit trail.
7. Commissioner changes one cycle note or deadline in the Access/Policy register and confirms the change is attributable.

## Exit criteria

- No role can see a draft or undistributed review record outside its permitted scope.
- No user can change another program's ballot or alter a finalized vote.
- Every stage change, access change, deadline change, moderation action, and decision has an actor and timestamp.
- A revised proposal exposes the current version, prior versions, and changed fields.
- A record packet can be downloaded and read without opening the portal.
- CSA confirms the configured quorum, threshold, proxy treatment, deadlines, and Board route.
