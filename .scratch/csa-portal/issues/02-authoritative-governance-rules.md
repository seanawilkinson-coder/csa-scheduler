# Establish the Authoritative CSA Governance Rules

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

Which governance source and effective date control each portal rule? Reconcile the current bylaws, the April 2026 Legislative Cycle v4 framework, earlier legislative drafts, the Coach Portal design, the All-Americans and Academic Awards documents, and coach feedback—especially legislative dates, co-sponsor count, vote thresholds, proxy legality, Board jurisdiction, award criteria, and exceptions.

## Answer

- The approved Legislative Cycle v4 framework is the governing product baseline for the 2026–27 season, including its proposal stages, deadlines, co-sponsor rule, vote thresholds, bucket jurisdiction, proxy process, Board process, feedback process, and emergency pathways.
- The approved v4 framework supersedes conflicting earlier legislative drafts for portal behavior. The current bylaws and any required amendments are treated as governance records and implementation constraints, not as a reason to implement the older draft behavior.
- The final approved All-Americans and Academic Awards documents are the current policy baselines for those modules.
- The Coach Questions & Responses Tracker is advisory input. Its suggestions and open questions do not alter portal behavior until CSA approves them separately.
- Policy refinements that are not yet approved remain future decisions; they must not be silently encoded in the build-ready specification.
