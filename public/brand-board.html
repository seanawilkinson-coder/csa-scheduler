The portal is the record

CSA Portal · Brand board

A serious record of shared decisions.

Make the annual legislative cycle legible. The portal should feel like the official record of a group that knows its rules, its deadlines, and the people responsible for each decision.

The cycle
Propose. Discuss. Decide.

A calm surface for proposals, revisions, comments, documents, votes, and Board outcomes.

AUGMAY

The thesis

Correctness is the floor. Clarity is the product.

The source documents describe a nine- to ten-month process with real participants, deadlines, revisions, comments, quorum, proxy rules, and Board authority. The interface gives that process a visible shape without turning it into an office spreadsheet.

The failure state

It looks like a spreadsheet.

No cells. No ruled matrix. No identical grey rows. No invented “insights” language. A proposal is a record with a route, a next action, and a history.

1 · Color jobs

Every color earns its place.

The base stays quiet. Navy carries trust and structure. Gold marks an active decision or earned attention. Maroon belongs to the association mark and urgent measures, not routine decoration.

3 · The cycle shape

The calendar is a rail, not a table.

The source documents describe an annual path from proposal submission through coaches, written comment, final discussion, and Board decision. Show the movement first.

4 · Surface language

Records have a shape.

A live proposal is not a “tile.” It is a line in the register: title, program, route, state, and next action. Open it to read the proposal, documents, history, and discussion in one continuous record.

5 · Voice and release gate

Use the language of the process.

The interface is not a narrator. It names what happened, who owns the next action, and what is allowed now.

Use

  • Annual Legislative Cycle
  • Proposal register
  • Comment window
  • Decision route
  • Record history
  • The portal is the record.

Reject

  • Governance insights
  • Workflow intelligence
  • Stakeholder engagement hub
  • AI-generated summary language
  • Rounded card mosaics and KPI tiles
  • Visible section numbers written as 01, 02, 03

Make the next decision obvious. Preserve the last one.
